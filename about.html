Built by people who missed home cooking.

Mannvasam started as a weekend experiment between three friends who kept ordering in because chopping vegetables after work felt impossible. This is that experiment, grown up.

Our Story

A kitchen problem, solved like engineers.

We didn't want another generic vegetable subscription. We wanted our grandmothers' sambar, without losing the evening to it.

Late 2024

The problem became personal

Three friends, three different kitchens, the same excuse every night: "too tired to cook properly." Ordering in had quietly become the default.

Early 2025

First kitchen test

We began pre-cutting vegetables for our own families' sambar and kuzhambu — timing the prep, measuring the waste, refining the ratio by hand.

Mid 2025

Sourcing partners in Kanchipuram

We partnered with local farms for same-day sourcing and built a small hygienic processing space around HACCP-aligned practice.

2026

Preparing to launch in Chennai

Five recipes, cold-chain delivery and a waitlist of early tasters — Mannvasam is getting ready for its first city.

What We Believe

Convenience shouldn't cost you the recipe.

Tradition first

Every kit follows a real family recipe — we don't simplify the method, only the prep.

Radical hygiene

Every batch is inspected, weighed and sealed under controlled, HACCP-aligned conditions.

Waste, minimised

Exact portions mean farmers sell what's needed and households throw away less.
